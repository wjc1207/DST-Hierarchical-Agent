Don't Starve Together planning reference (standard Wilson-style recipes)

This is strategic background, not live game state. Inventory, recipe availability,
nearby entities, and Lua's allowed actions must be checked from telemetry.
Character-specific recipes, world settings, and special circumstances may differ.

Core recipes (verified against this DST installation's scripts/recipes.lua):
- Torch: 2 cut grass (cutgrass) + 2 twigs (twigs); no science station required.
- Axe: 1 twig + 1 flint; no science station required.
- Pickaxe: 2 twigs + 2 flint; no science station required.
- Campfire: 3 cut grass + 2 logs; no science station required.
- Fire pit: 2 logs + 12 rocks; no science station required.
- Science Machine (recipe name researchlab): 1 gold nugget + 4 logs + 4 rocks;
  no science station required. This is the current longer-term milestone.
- Rope: 3 cut grass; normally requires a Science Machine to prototype.
- Spear: 2 twigs + 1 rope + 1 flint; normally requires a Science Machine.

Planning considerations:
- DeepSeek maintains a priority task_board with machine-checkable targets and
  real dependencies, not a strict action sequence. Complete visible feasible goals
  by priority; when none are visible, navigate for the highest ready goal. A missing
  higher-priority resource does not block gathering another visible board resource.
  Inventory counts mean CURRENT stock at completion, not additional gathering.
  Observed completed goals stay done after materials are consumed. Explicitly
  declare material/tool dependencies; the executor does not invent recipe tasks.
  Quarterly review is a patch, not a reset: preserve IDs, valid goals and progress;
  only cancel explicitly or revise with a reason based on changed facts. Time
  passing and task completion are not reasons to inflate stock targets.
  Each task has a bounded search time/distance; one route rejection does NOT prove
  a resource is absent. JEV handles safety, new off-board opportunities, and gaps.
- Runtime crafting only offers a new torch/axe/pickaxe when none is carried:
  request at most count=1, never a second reserve torch. Campfire placement is
  allowed only at night with no nearby lit fire. A dusk campfire task can wait
  while other visible goals proceed; it is not proof of missing materials.
- Klei room resource priors are supplied for OBSERVED topology room types. BGGrass
  can generate grass/saplings/berries, FlowerPatch primarily flowers/fireflies/bees,
  and BGRocky can generate rock1/rock2/flint. Identical ground tiles need not share
  resources. Priors are possible initial spawns, not guarantees, current stock or
  normalized probabilities. Layouts, functions and setting-dependent spawns may
  be missing from the extracted table. Actual observations always take precedence.
- A torch is portable light. A campfire or fire pit is stationary and does not
  light the route away from it. Secure light before darkness.
- Torch fuel is consumed while equipped. The native executor automatically unequips
  a torch auto-equipped by crafting during day or dusk, but keeps it at night.
- Judge portable-light reserves by remaining fuel and expected use, not count
  alone. inventory_details.items and inventory_details.equipped retain each
  item's durability_percent: for torches this is remaining fuel in [0,1], not a
  percentage in [0,100]. Missing fuel is unknown; do not assume full or empty.
  A nearly empty torch and a full torch are not equivalent reserves. Include
  both backpack and equipped torches without counting the equipped one twice.
- Local scripts/tuning.lua defines TORCH_FUEL = night_time * 1.25 with a base
  night_time of 2 * 30 seconds: a full torch has 75 base seconds of fuel.
  This is a reference, NOT guaranteed coverage of the current world's night.
  Rain increases torch drain; skills and other modifiers can change consumption.
  Seasons and world settings can change night length. Current telemetry gives
  phase/progress, not actual phase durations, weather, or active fuel modifiers;
  do not invent an exact remaining-night or effective-fuel duration from it.
- lighting.torch_equipped reports portable light in the hand. Each entry in
  lighting.observed_fires reports a campfire/firepit's observed distance and
  lit status (from the fire tag); null means unreported. A structure's presence
  alone does not mean it is burning. Remaining fire fuel/burn time is unknown.
  Lua uses an 8-unit nearby-lit-fire rule for torch switching, not a
  guarantee of safe illumination everywhere within that radius or all night.
- Reserve targets must correspond to a concrete expected shortage. Consider
  remaining torch fuel, stationary light, intended night travel, and the cost of
  consuming grass/twigs needed for other tasks. If current reserves cover the
  intended use with a justified margin, work on another unmet need. A completed
  target or new quarter is not a reason to turn 1 torch into 2, then 3, then 4.
  Extra crafting is allowed when needed, not a standing goal to maximize stock.
  No fixed torch-count cap applies: low fuel, changed conditions, or a longer
  trip can justify more, but state that reason using observed facts/uncertainty.
- Chopping and mining actions include bounded pickup near the completed target.
  Trees may yield logs and seeds (such as pinecones or acorns); mined rocks may
  yield rocks, flint, nitre, or gold, depending on the target.
- Source feasibility depends on prerequisites: without a usable pickaxe, a rock
  that can drop flint is NOT an executable way to get the flint needed to make
  that pickaxe. Seek loose flint or another obtainable source instead. A recipe
  being available does not mean its tool exists. Tools need enough remaining
  uses for a whole work target. Standard rock1 gives rocks/flint/nitre, not gold;
  rock2 is the standard gold-bearing boulder. Neither can be mined without a
  usable pickaxe. Loose gold/flint pickups do not require mining tools.
- A past resources_seen record is not proof of a currently obtainable source.
  The executor remembers failed region/product surveys instead of retrying an
  unchanged record after a timer. New obtainable-source evidence can restore
  its priority; an entrance, temporary hazard or path failure is not a complete
  survey. A need that persists does not itself make a failed source valid again.
- The eat_safe_food action can cook carried raw food at a nearby lit cooker and
  then eat it when needed. Do not plan a separate cook action solely for that.
- Exploration uses the game's topology IDs as region identity; ground tiles
  supply terrain/resource hints, not region boundaries. One topology region can
  contain mixed tiles; distinct regions can share a biome and get numbered names.
  explore_current_region searches locally. explore_other_region searches for
  NEW exits with a persistent outward heading and safe obstacle bypassing, not
  road following. Already discovering a neighbor does not disable new-exit
  search; completely observing the current boundary does.
- travel_region_<observed index> goes to a known graph node, whether newly
  discovered or previously visited; there is no separate return action. Use
  observed node_id values in visit_region or survey_region targets. Paths use verified passable
  gateways; the reported next-exit distance is not the final travel distance.
  Resources_seen are past observations, not guaranteed current stock. Terrain
  hints are only priors. No current action depends on following roads.
- Exploration and region travel are selected by the executor, not JEV. It uses
  priority goals, remembered source positions, known source-room records/BFS hops,
  Klei generation priors, and depth-first unknown-branch traversal with backtracking.
  JEV selects safety responses and worthwhile off-board opportunities; it is not
  asked about every plant or short movement leg.
  These are navigation policies, not a complete acquisition/tool-dependency solver.
- Exploration continues across short movement legs without requesting JEV
  after every leg. Ordinary resource events compare the SET of collectible,
  reachable product types, not entity GUIDs or counts: more grass alone is not
  a new type. Yield only while that set differs and displacement from execution
  start is at least 4 units. Threats, unsafe lighting, phase/region changes, and
  navigation failures can interrupt earlier; the 4-unit rule is not a safety delay.
- Oasis and ordinary desert share desert ground cues; do not infer an oasis
  without additional evidence. Prioritize resources actually observed nearby.
- Terrain is only a search hint: forest often has trees and saplings; grassland
  often has grass and saplings; savanna often has grass and may have beefalo;
  rocky ground often has rocks and flint; swamp can contain reeds and hostile
  creatures. World generation varies, so never assume an unseen resource is
  present or safe to reach. For a needed material, prefer a biome likely to
  contain it, then verify via the live nearby-entity observations.
- PotentialThreat means a nearby hostile may attack on approach even while it
  is not actively chasing. Avoid revisiting a recently observed enemy location
  after fleeing; navigation remembers it briefly and filters unsafe legs.
- Prefer feasible resource prerequisites and immediate survival over an invented
  objective. A planned recipe is not necessarily currently craftable.
